## Notes

- `categoryMoney` deliberately means low-stakes everyday spending, not investment or borrowing advice.
- `percentageCaveat` and `luckyTimesCaveat` must remain close to the displayed percentages.
- Birth-country strings remain translated for now because the engine currently uses birth-country/timezone information. The language selector does not decide that separate product change.
