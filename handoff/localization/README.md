# AstraCue localization handoff (content owner: Codex; implementation owner: Claude)

This directory is **editorial input**, not Flutter source. Claude may consume or copy approved strings into the app, but Codex owns wording and locale parity. `EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md` records the self-review and integration status. Current target locales, in product order: English (`en`, initial/default), Vietnamese (`vi`), Japanese (`ja`), Spanish (`es`, neutral international), Thai (`th`), Hindi for India (`hi-IN`), Simplified Chinese for mainland China (`zh-Hans-CN`). India and China have other languages/scripts; these two additions do not imply coverage of every resident. English is the fallback for a missing key, but a locale must not be declared release-ready while visible English fallback remains.

## Contract for implementation

1. Keep engine/API identifiers unchanged: `general`, `love`, `career`, `money`, `study`, `friends`, `other`; the seven decision-mode wire values; period wire values; energy levels; color keys; statuses and formula outputs. Translate only presentation. Do not translate persisted data or the calculation engine.
2. The Home description deck persists a **0-based index** into the 30 English sentences. The daily-energy deck persists a **0-based index** within an eight-sentence tone pool. Translate by those stable IDs (`homeDescription00`…`29`, `energyQuiet00`…`07`, etc.), preserving order and pool sizes. Do not pick a different sentence when locale changes. Prefer returning an ID from the deck and resolving the localized text at display time. Preserve existing saved deck state and cooldown behavior.
3. Results and history may be reopened after a language switch. Render mode, category, period, energy level, colors and status from stable keys in the saved reading; never trust the English `name` or `displayLabel` as the translated surface. The shade's key/hex remain canonical.
4. Use locale-aware date/time/decimal formatting. Never concatenate translated fragments to form a sentence, especially `Your Luckiest Times This ${period.label}` or `This ${period}`. A 52.2% symbolic score is **not** a probability; preserve this distinction in every locale. Never localize a score by altering its numeric value.
5. Text inside Semantics labels, tooltips, SnackBars, errors, share text, onboarding, history and Responsible Use is in scope. `YES/NO`, `LEFT/RIGHT`, etc. are symbolic pairs, not physical navigation or instructions. Screen-reader reading order and 48dp touch targets must stay intact.
6. Use English as the default on first launch as requested. On the **existing welcome screen**, above the profile step, show an always-visible globe + current language selector; do not open an unsolicited startup popup or add a separate required screen. Tapping it opens a language bottom sheet listing native names (`English`, `Tiếng Việt`, `日本語`, `Español`, `ไทย`, `हिन्दी`, `简体中文`). Selection immediately rerenders the welcome screen and all subsequent onboarding, date/time pickers, safety text and app screens in that language; persist the choice before profile creation. Allow changing it later from an obvious Home/profile settings entry. A phone's country/location/timezone only affects readings, never language. Do not add machine translation or a third-party translation service at runtime.
7. Test `vi`, `ja`, `es`, `th`, `hi-IN`, and `zh-Hans-CN` on a narrow Android screen (360dp) and at 1.3–1.5 text scale. Avoid truncating safety copy. Test first launch, language switch on Welcome/Profile/Home/Result/History, app restart, past-day result, and a reading with `balanced`, `insufficientData`, or `periodElapsed` status. Do not ship mixed-language screens.
8. The app currently declares no bundled font family in `pubspec.yaml` or `theme.dart`. Android's system fallback may cover Han and Devanagari, but is device-dependent; that is not a proof of glyph coverage. Claude should use a tested font stack or licensed bundled fonts with Simplified Chinese and Devanagari coverage, register the weights actually used, and verify real Android renders. Do not force the English all-caps/large-letter-spacing treatment onto Hindi or Chinese. Increase line height where vowel marks/descenders clip; allow wrapping instead of ellipsis for safety and onboarding text. Preserve Unicode strings end-to-end; no transliteration of Hindi/Chinese into Latin letters.
9. Language and birthplace are separate fields. Adding the language selector does **not** authorize removing the birth-country input or inferring birthplace from language. The product decision about replacing birth country with an explicitly confirmed birth timezone remains open and must be handled separately, with calculation-coverage tests.

## Editorial tone

Mystical but measured: invite reflection, curiosity and calm. Do not promise certainty, luck, success, a true physical energy measurement, or divine proof. The result is a symbolic alignment/direction, never a command. Japanese should sound natural and gentle rather than literal; Spanish should avoid country-specific slang; Thai should use clear contemporary language and avoid overly formal royal/religious register. Hindi should use clear contemporary standard Hindi without forced Sanskritization; Simplified Chinese should use natural mainland phrasing without turning symbolic “signals” into guarantees. The brand name `AstraCue` stays unchanged.

## Scope split / acceptance

- **Codex**: English source wording, Vietnamese/Japanese/Spanish/Thai/Hindi/Simplified Chinese translations, term glossary, exact key parity, editorial QA. This handoff stays outside `mobile_app/lib` until Claude integrates it.
- **Claude**: Flutter localization setup, string extraction and key wiring, locale picker/persistence, formatting, presentation-key mapping, responsive layout, tests and APK. Claude must send a missing-key list to Codex; do not invent translations for an absent key or silently ship English in a translated locale.
- **Joint sign-off**: all visible strings covered; no formula changes; stable deck behavior; no mixed-language screens; safety meaning preserved; narrow Android layout verified.

## Global safety issue to resolve before release

The current crisis-support copy hardcodes Vietnam/US phone numbers. Do **not** reuse those as a global fallback for Japan, Spain, Thailand, India or China. `SAFETY_COPY.md` supplies a neutral global replacement and removes the existing “at least 13” / “100% personal responsibility” claims from prototype copy. This is an editorial decision, not a certification of store-release age/privacy/legal compliance. No user-location inference for language is needed.

## Delivery status

Core-screen copy has 70 matching keys across all seven locales (`CORE_COPY.md`, `CORE_COPY_VI.md`, `CORE_COPY_HI_ZH.md`). The rotating bank has 30 Home descriptions + 8 messages for each of eight energy tones = 94 matching keys in each `rotation_*.json` file; keys and nonempty values have been checked, with no exact duplicate sentence in a locale. `SELECTOR_AND_TERMS.md` covers language selection, choice labels, energy labels, and the 20 color names. `LUCKY_TIMES_HEADINGS.md` replaces the grammatically unsafe period interpolation with four complete headings. `LOADING_COPY.md` covers loading narration and orbit labels. `SAFETY_COPY.md` contains the editorially approved prototype safety copy, including replacements for the old age/“100% responsibility” and hard-coded crisis-number sentences. These are editorial drafts, not native-speaker or store-release legal sign-off. Remaining before any non-English release: error/status variants, share/history/accessibility details, and an exhaustive source-string parity audit. No Flutter code, build, or Git push is included in this handoff.
