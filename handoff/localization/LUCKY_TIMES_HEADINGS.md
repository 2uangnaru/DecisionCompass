# Complete luckiest-time headings

Use one complete localized heading for the selected non-`now` period. Do **not** interpolate a chip label into a sentence; “in Morning” and equivalents are ungrammatical. `now` has no lucky-window heading. Keep `luckyTimesCaveat` immediately under the windows and never treat the displayed percent as an outcome probability.
